# Show operational truth

Read [console requirements](../../../docs/product/console.md). Keep application/environment and production risk visible. Use server-computed capabilities; UI hiding is not access control.

Distinguish queued, running, review required, delivered, failed, and unknown outcome. A 202 is acceptance, not success. Separate public replies from internal notes and show recipients.

Show source/version and verified screenshots beside the correct steps. Actor badges identify who may act now, not guessed permissions. Approval screens display the exact target, inputs, evidence, risk, and expiry.

Test keyboard use, labels/focus, responsive evidence viewing, unauthorized deep links, error states, and stale capability updates. Do not invent working product screens over placeholder endpoints.
